2. Purpose and audience
Gotchaaaa connects event organizers, businesses and associations with event service providers, including for urgent needs. Organizers publish missions, search profiles and availability, review proposals and manage bookings. Providers manage profiles and availability, find missions and submit proposals. Both roles exchange messages. The marketplace helps users find suitable event professionals quickly and coordinate their services.
